# Storytelling and motion

The first journey moves from the homepage project index to the Agents case
study: the problem, the choice of a familiar taskbar surface, the interaction
and its states, platform constraints, and the evidence and scope of the work.

The homepage showcase uses native scrolling and anchor links. On tall desktop
viewports, each project has a short sticky chapter; on smaller viewports and
with reduced motion it remains a normal list. A scroll-linked image reveal is
progressively enhanced where supported. The case-study link navigates directly,
using the same image on both sides rather than introducing a second transition.
The taskbar sequence offers direct controls with all three explanations visible;
the existing simulation remains illustrative and runs locally.

## Artifact gaps

- **Decision alternatives:** cleared Figma frames for app-bound, independent pin,
  and dedicated space concepts, plus notes describing their tradeoffs. The
  existing decision table remains the evidence until those are available.
- **Taskbar progression:** matched captures of an empty pin, active state and
  expanded card from the same product version. The current steps combine
  concept crops and a still from the existing taskbar recording.
- **Multiple agents:** a cleared recording of two agents with one requesting
  input. The existing recording slot describes the desired shots; it is not
  evidence of an available recording.
- **Invocation and outcome:** a verified recording showing selection, dispatch,
  and return to the taskbar, and public launch or preview references with dates
  and availability. The existing stage photograph documents a preview, not
  adoption or general availability.

For each supplied artifact, confirm its source, permission for public use,
product version and whether it is a concept, prototype, preview or release.
Provide a legible poster for every video and avoid private data in captures.

## Future use

Recall and Teams can use the same project chapter and direct index patterns;
apply a step sequence only where existing evidence shows an actual sequence.
About should remain a quieter reading experience. Any shared change must be
checked on all published routes, including reduced-motion and narrow layouts.
